# DiskLens 1.0.12

The Map tab now offers Explorer table (default), Ranked sizes, and Space map. The picker preserves the current folder and selected item without querying or rescanning. Explorer supports sorting by name, numeric size, and type; ranked sizes orders largest first and emphasizes proportional bars.

View and comfortable/compact density preferences persist independently of light/dark theme. Tables support Enter or double-click to open folders or reveal files. Space map uses single-click selection and double-click to open; Open selected works across views. Existing indexed-item limits and the explicit remainder are shared by all three views.
